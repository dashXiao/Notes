# Notes

A personal memo and scheduling assistant. Capture your thoughts, save spoken ideas as notes, and turn everyday plans into schedules.

A native iOS app built with SwiftUI.

## Demo

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/94ddf817-2fc4-4ab7-8c89-280cca427535

## What You Can Do

1. **Capture thoughts effortlessly.** Jot down a note whenever inspiration strikes, or speak in English and have your words transcribed and saved automatically.
2. **Turn plans into schedules with AI.** AI recognizes plans in your notes and automatically saves them as schedule entries, ready to review or edit.

## Quick Start

Requires **Xcode 26.6**.

1. Copy the configuration template:

   ```sh
   cp Config/Secrets.xcconfig.example Config/Secrets.xcconfig
   ```

2. Set `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY` in `Config/Secrets.xcconfig` to your DeepSeek API key. If you don't have one, [create one here](https://platform.deepseek.com/api_keys).
